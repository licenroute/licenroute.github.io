Paste the prompt

Your assistant reads your code and suggests what to charge for. Login, health checks and writes stay free.

Paste this into Claude Code or Cursor

Add paid access to my Express API with LicenRoute.
Read licenroute.com/llms.txt first and follow its rules.
Suggest which routes should be paid and which stay free.
Show me the exact change. I approve it in my terminal.
Run the LicenRoute tests and explain the results simply.

2

Type the code

A short code in your terminal approves the exact change. It’s the one step your assistant can’t do for you.

LicenRoute is going to write:
  modify server.js
  modify .gitignore
  plans: /api/catalogo/**, /api/informes/**
  code: apr_…
If you approve, type 5QPE-95MJ and press Enter (anything else cancels): 5QPE-95MJ
Approval confirmed.
LicenRoute apply — applied (server.js, .gitignore).

Before you paste it

    

      
Do I need to understand the code?
Not deeply. You read a short summary and approve it yourself, and your assistant can explain each step.

      
Will my login break?
No. Login, health checks and sign-up are never made paid, and the tests check that free pages still work.

      
Can I charge real money?
Yes, by Bizum or bank transfer: a buyer requests a pack, you send your payment details, register the payment and they collect the key. Card payments come after a security review. How selling works.
