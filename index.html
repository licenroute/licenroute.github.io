In plain words

What changes in your app

You choose what's paid — for example your reports or your data. Everything else keeps working for free: sign-up, login, your home page.

Someone calls a paid part of your API…What they get
without a key402 “Pay first” — plus which plan to buy and where
with a valid key200 They get in. One use is counted
with a key for a different plan402 Buying one thing doesn't unlock everything
with a made-up key403 Refused. Keys can't be guessed
after using up their package (e.g. 1,000 calls)402 Time to buy another package
after you revoke their key403 Out, instantly. No redeploy
while LicenRoute itself is down503 on paid parts — nothing is given away for free. Free parts keep working

Why it's safe with AI

Your assistant does the work. You keep the keys.

It can't approve itself

Writing to your code needs a code typed by a person in the terminal. No setting, no shortcut, no prompt can skip it.

It can't read your secrets

An optional add-on for Claude Code and Cursor stops the assistant from opening your .env files and from running unknown packages.

AI agents understand the answer

When a bot or AI agent hits a paid part, the “pay first” answer is machine-readable: plan, price and where to buy. They can pay instead of scraping.

See it working

Try it on an example app in 5 minutes

No app of your own needed. You need Node.js 24 or newer, npm and git. No account, no Docker. Or just ask your assistant to do it with you.

git clone https://github.com/licenroute/licenroute.git licenroute && cd licenroute
npm ci --ignore-scripts
npm run build
./demo/demo.sh          # shows what it would change, without changing anything

Then follow the 10-minute tutorial to approve it and run the tests yourself.

Never run npx licenroute. LicenRoute is not on npm yet, and a package with that name there is not this project. Your assistant knows this if it reads llms.txt.

FAQ

Questions

I built my app with AI. Can I really use this?

If your backend is a Node.js app using Express (ask your assistant: “is my backend Express?”), yes. Paste the prompt and your assistant does the setup. You read the summary and approve it in your terminal.

Do I need to understand the code?

Not deeply. LicenRoute shows a summary of what it will change, and your assistant can explain it. What you can't skip is approving it yourself — that's what keeps your app safe.

Can I charge real money today?

No. The preview runs in demo mode on your computer, so you can test everything risk-free. Real keys and payments need the hosted service, which isn't open yet. If you'd use it, tell us.

Will it break my login or my free pages?

No. LicenRoute never proposes making login, health checks or sign-up paid. It lists them so you can see they stay free, and its tests check that free pages keep working.

What if LicenRoute goes down?

Paid parts close (nobody gets in for free) and free parts keep working. The tests simulate exactly that.

Does it cost anything?

The preview is free and open source (Apache-2.0).

For developers

The technical details

What it is

A CLI plus an Express 4/5 middleware. The CLI analyses your app statically (it never runs it), proposes plans per route prefix and HTTP method (GET+HEAD share quota, OPTIONS is never charged) and writes only the diff bound to an approval code plus a one-time challenge typed on /dev/tty. Node's permission model is a second barrier.

What clients receive

Without a licence: 402 with {"error":"payment_required","payment":{"plan_id","checkout_url","currency","unit_price_minor","unit_size"}}. Licences go in Authorization: Bearer bgat_…. Paid routes fail closed (503) if the decision service is unreachable, even at startup.

Packages

@licenroute/cli 0.3.1 and @licenroute/connect-express 0.8.0, built from source or downloaded from the release and verified against SHA256SUMS. Node.js 24+ for the CLI, 20+ for the SDK.
