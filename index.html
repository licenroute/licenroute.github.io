2

Type the code

A short code in your terminal approves the exact change. It’s the one step your assistant can’t do for you.

LicenRoute is going to write:
  modify server.js
  modify .gitignore
  plans: /api/catalogo/**, /api/informes/**
  code: apr_…
If you approve, type 5QPE-95MJ and press Enter (anything else cancels): 5QPE-95MJ
Approval confirmed.
LicenRoute apply — applied (server.js, .gitignore).

Agents can read the bill

      
Bots and AI agents get a 402 that names the plan, the price and the checkout link. They can pay instead of scraping.

Before you paste it

    

      
Do I need to understand the code?
Not deeply. You read a short summary and approve it yourself, and your assistant can explain each step.

      
Will my login break?
No. Login, health checks and sign-up are never made paid, and the tests check that free pages still work.

      
Can I charge real money?
Not yet. The hosted pilot issues real licences and enforces quotas, but payments stay in Stripe test mode until a security review.
