LicenRoute user manual

Publish your API, charge for it in your own Stripe account, and let people and AI agents try and buy it.

The panel (app.licenroute.com) and the Market are online in a pilot; payments run in Stripe test mode, with no real money. The screenshots come from a local demo with sample data, and the app's interface is in Spanish.

What LicenRoute is

LicenRoute lets you charge for your API without building billing: each buyer gets a key with its quota, and you get paid in your own Stripe account.

Getting started in the panel

Sign in with your email and password; the panel opens on your organisation's Summary.

  1. Open the panel and enter your email and password.
  2. The Summary shows how many sites, active API keys and members your organisation has, and the first three steps.
  3. Use the top menu to move around.

The button with your organisation's name, top right, switches organisation if you belong to several.

Registering your API and its plans

Each API is a site with its domain; inside the site you define the plans you sell: which routes charge, at what price and with how much quota.

The site

  1. Go to Sitios and press Nuevo sitio: name, your API's domain and the consent.
  2. On the site page, create an API key (shown only once) and copy the three environment variables to your server.
  3. Usually your AI assistant makes the connection with the prompt from licenroute.com; you only approve the change in your terminal.

The plans

The site's Licencias tab lists its plans; press Editar on one or create another.

The price buyers see is computed: quota × price. It is never typed by hand.

Getting paid in your Stripe account

Sales are charged in your own Stripe account: the money goes straight to you, and LicenRoute never receives or holds it.

  1. Go to Cobros and press Conectar mi cuenta de Stripe. Only the organisation's owner can do it.
  2. Stripe asks for your details and your bank's. If you stop halfway, come back and press Continuar en Stripe.
  3. On your return the panel asks Stripe and shows the status. With lista para cobrar (ready to charge), your instant-purchase plans show the Comprar (Buy) button in the Market.
StatusMeaning
No connected accountYour plans cannot be bought directly; buyers can only request access
Onboarding not finishedStripe still needs details
Submitted; Stripe is reviewing itStripe does not allow charges yet
Ready to chargeSales are charged in your account

Stripe's fees are charged to your account like any other payment of yours. Comprobar estado en Stripe checks the status again whenever you want.

Publishing your API in the Market

The listing is what buyers see: only what you write in it, the domain and the plans you choose. It never shows your routes, limits, keys or policies.

  1. On the site, open the Market tab and press Nueva ficha.
  2. Fill in title, publisher, category, summary and description.
  3. Add the documentation (for example your /openapi.json), the service status if you have one, and an example request and response.
  4. Tick the plans you offer and choose the visibility.
  5. Save. With Pública visibility the listing appears in the catalogue.
VisibilityWho sees it
HiddenNobody
Link onlyWhoever has the private link (it can be regenerated)
PublicAnyone: the catalogue, catalog.json and licenroute.com/apis

In the example write bgat_… instead of a key: listings refuse keys with a real format. The public page address (for example festivos-es) comes from the title if you leave it empty. The listing also shows the requests served in the last 30 days, from real usage.

Serving your buyers

With instant purchase you do nothing: the buyer pays and gets the key. You only step in for plans with your approval, or to review or cut off access.

Market requests

  1. Open Solicitudes Market: pending requests first; filter by approved, rejected or withdrawn.
  2. Press Revisar to see who is asking, whether they verified their email, and why.
  3. Aprobar opens a Stripe payment for the buyer; the key is delivered only once the payment shows as paid. Rechazar closes the request. Either way you can leave a note, and the buyer gets an email.

Buyers and licences

Compradores lists everyone holding your licences, paid or trial. Open one to see their licences, the usage of each and the button to revoke them. A revoked licence stops working at once, without redeploying your API. A suspended buyer cannot buy or try your APIs, and is not told why.

Buyer's guide

In the Market you find an API, try it for free and buy it in a minute. The key you get is sent with every request as Authorization: Bearer bgat_….

Finding an API

The Catálogo lists public APIs, with search and a category filter. A listing shows what the API does, an example request and response, its documentation, the requests served in 30 days and its plans with the final price.

Creating your account

  1. Press Crear cuenta: name, email, a password of 12 characters or more, and accept the terms.
  2. Open the link in the email to verify the account. Unverified accounts cannot try, buy or request access.
  3. Sign in with your email and password.

Free trial and purchase

Once signed in, each plan on the listing shows its buttons:

ButtonWhat happens
Probar gratis (Try for free)You get a key at once with the trial quota (for example 50 requests for 7 days). One trial per plan, up to 3 a day
Comprar (Buy)You go to Stripe's payment page. After paying you come back to a page that hands you the key once
Solicitar acceso (Request access)For plans with approval: you explain what you need it for and the owner decides. If approved, you pay and get the key

Copy the key as soon as you see it: it is not shown again, because LicenRoute only stores a hash of it. If you lose it, the owner can revoke it and you get another one.

Your purchases, trials and requests

Mis compras shows each payment with its status (pending, paid, refunded…) and each trial with its usage. Mis solicitudes shows your access requests and the owner's decision. In Mi cuenta you change your details, download your data or delete the account.

For AI agents

AI assistants can find and recommend Market APIs in two ways. Neither pays anything: the agent suggests and a person decides.

1. The catalogue as JSON. GET /market/catalog.json returns the public listings with their plans, prices, quotas and trials, in the licenroute.market.catalog/v1 format. It has no internal routes, limits or keys.

2. The MCP server. packages/market-mcp has no dependencies and offers three tools:

ToolWhat it returns
search_apisAPIs that match what you ask for ("spanish holidays", "validate NIF"), with the lowest price
get_apiEverything about one API: description, example, real usage and plans
request_purchaseThe link where a person starts the trial or buys

To connect it in Claude Code:

claude mcp add licenroute-market -e LICENROUTE_MARKET_URL=https://<your-market>/market -- node <path>/packages/market-mcp/bin/licenroute-market-mcp.mjs

In Cursor, add the same to .cursor/mcp.json; the package README has the example.

Questions and current limits

Is real money charged? No. Everything runs in Stripe test mode today: you can pay with test cards and no money moves.

Can I use it with real customers now? In a pilot: the panel issues real licences and enforces quotas, and buyers can try APIs for free. Charging real money comes after the security review.

What if LicenRoute does not answer? Your API's paid routes return 503 until it is back; public routes keep working.

I lost my key. It cannot be recovered: only a hash is stored. Ask the owner to revoke it and get another one.

What does a paid route answer? No key: 402 with the plan and the purchase link. Valid key: 200. Invented or revoked key: 403. Quota used up: 402.

Which technologies are supported? Node.js APIs with Express 4 or 5. For other frameworks, tell us on licenroute.com.
