Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 26 March: Good Friday, a three-day weekend.
  • 29 March: Easter Monday, a three-day weekend.
  • 27 May: Corpus Christi is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 18 June: National Holiday, a three-day weekend.
  • 29 June: Independence Day is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 1 November: All Saints' Day, a three-day weekend.
