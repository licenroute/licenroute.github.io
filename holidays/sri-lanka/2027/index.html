Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 15 January: Tamil Thai Pongal Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 1 February: Navam Full Moon Poya Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 4 February: National Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 15 February: Mahasivarathri Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 2 March: Madin Full Moon Poya Day is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 26 March: Good Friday, a three-day weekend.
  • 1 April: Bak Full Moon Poya Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 13 April: Day before Sinhala and Tamil New Year is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 28 May: Id-Ul-Alha (Hadji Festival Day), a three-day weekend.
  • 29 June: Poson Full Moon Poya Day is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 29 July: Esala Full Moon Poya Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 26 August: Milad-Un-Nabi (Holy Prophet's Birthday) is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 27 August: Nikini Full Moon Poya Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 25 October: Vap Full Moon Poya Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 8 November: Deepavali Festival Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 23 December: Unduvap Full Moon Poya Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
