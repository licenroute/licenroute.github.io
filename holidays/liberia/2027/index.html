Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 11 February: Armed Forces Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 15 March: J. J. Robert's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 9 April: Fast and Prayer Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 14 May: National Unification Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 26 July: Independence Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 24 August: Flag Day is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 4 November: Thanksgiving is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 29 November: William Tubman's Birthday, a three-day weekend.
