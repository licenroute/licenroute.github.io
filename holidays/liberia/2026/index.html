Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 10 April: Fast and Prayer Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 14 May: National Unification Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 24 August: Flag Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 5 November: Thanksgiving is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 25 December: Christmas Day, a three-day weekend.
