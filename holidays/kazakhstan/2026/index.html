Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 2 January: New Year's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 9 March: International Women's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 16 March: Constitution Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 23 March: Nauryz, a three-day weekend.
  • 24 March: Nauryz is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 1 May: People's Unity Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 7 May: Defender of the Fatherland Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 11 May: Victory Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 6 July: Capital City Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 26 October: Republic Day, a three-day weekend.
