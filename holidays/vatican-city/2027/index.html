Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: Solemnity of Mary, Mother of God, a three-day weekend.
  • 11 February: Anniversary of the foundation of Vatican City is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 19 March: Saint Joseph, a three-day weekend.
  • 25 March: Maundy Thursday is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 26 March: Good Friday, a three-day weekend.
  • 29 March: Easter Monday, a three-day weekend.
  • 30 March: In Albis Tuesday is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 6 May: Ascension Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 27 May: Corpus Christi is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 29 June: Saints Peter and Paul is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 16 August: Summer holiday, a three-day weekend.
  • 17 September: Saint Robert Bellarmine - Name day of the Holy Father, a three-day weekend.
  • 1 November: All Saints' Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 2 November: All Souls' Day is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 27 December: St. John, a three-day weekend.
  • 31 December: New Year's Eve, a three-day weekend.
