Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 5 January: Laylat al-Mi'raj is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 9 March: End of Ramadan (Eid al-Fitr) is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 18 March: Cheikh Al Maarouf Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 6 July: Independence Day is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 12 November: Maore Day, a three-day weekend.
