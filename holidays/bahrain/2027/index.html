Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 9 March: End of Ramadan (Eid al-Fitr) is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 15 June: Day of Ashura is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 16 December: National Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 17 December: Accession Day, a three-day weekend.
