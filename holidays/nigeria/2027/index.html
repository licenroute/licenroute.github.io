Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 9 March: Id el Fitr is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 26 March: Good Friday, a three-day weekend.
  • 29 March: Easter Monday, a three-day weekend.
  • 3 May: Worker's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 27 May: Children's Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 14 June: Democracy Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 1 October: Independence Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 1 November: National Youth Day, a three-day weekend.
