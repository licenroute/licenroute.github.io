  • 5 January: Epiphany, a three-day weekend.
  • 26 January: Duarte's Birthday, a three-day weekend.
  • 27 February: Independence Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 3 April: Good Friday, a three-day weekend.
  • 4 May: Labour Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 4 June: Corpus Christi is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 24 September: Our Lady of las Mercedes is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
