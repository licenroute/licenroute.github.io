Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 26 January: Australia Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 17 February: Chinese New Year is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 20 March: Hari Raya Puasa / Labour Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 6 April: Easter Monday / Self Determination Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 16 June: Islamic New Year is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 25 August: Hari Maulaud Nabi is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 25 December: Christmas Day, a three-day weekend.
