Long weekends and bridges

  • 5 January: Mabaas of Muhammad is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 11 February: Anniversary of Islamic Revolution is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 9 March: End of Ramadan (Eid al-Fitr) is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 22 March: Nowruz, a three-day weekend.
  • 23 March: Nowruz is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 1 April: Islamic Republic Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 2 April: Martyrdom of Imam Jafar / Sizdah Bedar, a three-day weekend.
  • 24 May: Eid al-Ghadeer, a three-day weekend.
  • 4 June: Imam Khomeini's Demise, a three-day weekend.
  • 14 June: Tasoua, a three-day weekend.
  • 15 June: Ashoura is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 3 August: Martyrdom of Imam Reza is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 10 August: Martyrdom of Imam Hassan is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 19 August: Birthday of Muhammad Prophet is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 2 November: Martyrdom of Fatima-Zahara is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
