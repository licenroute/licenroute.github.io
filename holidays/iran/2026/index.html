Long weekends and bridges

  • 2 January: Birthday of Imam Ali, a three-day weekend.
  • 16 January: Mabaas of Muhammad, a three-day weekend.
  • 3 February: Birthday of Imam Mahdi is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 10 March: Martyrdom of Imam Ali is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 20 March: End of Ramadan (Eid al-Fitr) / Nationalization of the Iranian oil industry, a three-day weekend.
  • 23 March: Nowruz, a three-day weekend.
  • 24 March: Nowruz is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 2 April: Sizdah Bedar is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 13 April: Martyrdom of Imam Jafar, a three-day weekend.
  • 4 June: Eid al-Ghadeer / Imam Khomeini's Demise is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 5 June: Revolt of Khordad 15, a three-day weekend.
  • 25 June: Ashoura is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 3 August: Arbaeen-e Hosseini, a three-day weekend.
  • 11 August: Demise of Prophet Muhammad and Imam Hassan (Mujtaba) is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 13 August: Martyrdom of Imam Reza is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 21 August: Martyrdom of Imam Hassan, a three-day weekend.
  • 13 November: Martyrdom of Fatima-Zahara, a three-day weekend.
  • 22 December: Birthday of Imam Ali is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
