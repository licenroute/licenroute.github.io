Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 4 January: New Year Holiday, a three-day weekend.
  • 9 March: End of Ramadan (Eid al-Fitr) is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 15 March: Summer Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 22 March: Sultan Nevruz's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 29 March: Catholic Easter, a three-day weekend.
  • 3 May: Labour Day / Orthodox Easter, a three-day weekend.
  • 19 October: Mother Teresa Day is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 29 November: Independence Day / Liberation Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 27 December: Christmas Day, a three-day weekend.
