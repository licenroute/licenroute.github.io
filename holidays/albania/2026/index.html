Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 2 January: New Year Holiday, a three-day weekend.
  • 16 March: Summer Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 20 March: End of Ramadan (Eid al-Fitr), a three-day weekend.
  • 23 March: Sultan Nevruz's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 6 April: Catholic Easter, a three-day weekend.
  • 13 April: Orthodox Easter, a three-day weekend.
  • 1 May: Labour Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 19 October: Mother Teresa Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 30 November: Independence Day / Liberation Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 8 December: Youth Day is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 25 December: Christmas Day, a three-day weekend.
