Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 8 February: Shrove Monday, a three-day weekend.
  • 9 February: Shrove Tuesday is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 26 March: Good Friday, a three-day weekend.
  • 2 April: Day of the Veterans and the Fallen in the Malvinas War, a three-day weekend.
  • 25 May: Day of the May Revolution is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 21 June: Anniversary of the Passing to Immortality of General Martín Miguel de Güemes, a three-day weekend.
  • 9 July: Independence Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 16 August: Anniversary of the Passing to Immortality of General José de San Martín, a three-day weekend.
  • 11 October: Day of Respect for Cultural Diversity, a three-day weekend.
