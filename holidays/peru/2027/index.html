Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 25 March: Maundy Thursday is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 26 March: Good Friday, a three-day weekend.
  • 7 June: Battle of Arica and Flag Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 29 June: Saints Peter and Paul is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 23 July: Peruvian Air Force Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 29 July: Independence Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 6 August: Battle of Junín, a three-day weekend.
  • 30 August: Santa Rosa de Lima, a three-day weekend.
  • 8 October: Battle of Angamos, a three-day weekend.
  • 1 November: All Saints' Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 9 December: Battle of Ayacucho is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
