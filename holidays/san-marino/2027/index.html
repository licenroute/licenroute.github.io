Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 5 February: Feast of Saint Agatha, a three-day weekend.
  • 25 March: Anniversary of the Arengo is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 29 March: Easter Monday, a three-day weekend.
  • 1 April: Inauguration Ceremony is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 27 May: Corpus Christi is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 3 September: The Feast of San Marino and the Republic, a three-day weekend.
  • 1 October: Cerimonia di investitura dei Capitani Reggenti, a three-day weekend.
  • 1 November: All Saints' Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 2 November: Commemoration of the deceased is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
