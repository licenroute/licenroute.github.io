Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 23 February: Republic Day is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 26 March: Good Friday, a three-day weekend.
  • 29 March: Easter Monday, a three-day weekend.
  • 5 July: CARICOM Day, a three-day weekend.
