Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 6 January: Epiphany is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 23 February: Clean Monday, a three-day weekend.
  • 10 April: Good Friday, a three-day weekend.
  • 13 April: Easter Monday, a three-day weekend.
  • 1 May: Labour Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 1 June: Whit Monday, a three-day weekend.
  • 25 December: Christmas Day, a three-day weekend.
