Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 9 February: Korean New Year is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 1 March: Independence Movement Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 13 May: Buddha's Birthday is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 19 July: Constitution Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 16 August: Liberation Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 14 September: Korean Thanksgiving is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 4 October: National Foundation Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 11 October: Hangul Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 27 December: Christmas Day, a three-day weekend.
