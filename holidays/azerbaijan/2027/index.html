Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 4 January: New Year's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 8 March: International Women's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 9 March: End of Ramadan (Eid al-Fitr) is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 10 May: Day of Victory over Fascism, a three-day weekend.
  • 17 May: Feast of the Sacrifice (Eid al-Adha), a three-day weekend.
  • 18 May: Feast of the Sacrifice (Eid al-Adha) is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 28 May: Republic Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 15 June: National Salvation Day is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 28 June: Azerbaijan Armed Forces Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 18 October: Independence Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 9 November: Flag Day is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 12 November: Constitution Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 31 December: International Solidarity Day of Azerbaijanis, a three-day weekend.
