Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 2 January: New Year's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 9 March: International Women's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 20 March: End of Ramadan (Eid al-Fitr) / Spring Festival, a three-day weekend.
  • 23 March: End of Ramadan (Eid al-Fitr), a three-day weekend.
  • 11 May: Day of Victory over Fascism, a three-day weekend.
  • 28 May: Feast of the Sacrifice (Eid al-Adha) / Republic Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 15 June: National Salvation Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 26 June: Azerbaijan Armed Forces Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 19 October: Independence Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 9 November: Flag Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 12 November: Constitution Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 17 November: National Revival Day is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 31 December: International Solidarity Day of Azerbaijanis is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
