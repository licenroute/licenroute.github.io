Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 16 February: Spring Festival, a three-day weekend.
  • 17 February: Spring Festival is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 1 May: Labour Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 4 May: Youth Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 1 June: Children's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 19 June: Dragon Boat Festival, a three-day weekend.
  • 25 September: Mid-Autumn Festival, a three-day weekend.
  • 1 October: National Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 2 October: National Day, a three-day weekend.
