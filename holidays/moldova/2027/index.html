Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 7 January: Orthodox Christmas is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 8 March: International Women's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 3 May: Easter Monday, a three-day weekend.
  • 10 May: Memorial Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 27 August: Independence Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 31 August: National Language Day is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
