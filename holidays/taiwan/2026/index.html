Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: Founding of the Republic of China is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 16 February: Chinese New Year's Eve, a three-day weekend.
  • 17 February: Chinese New Year is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 19 February: The third day of the Chinese New Year is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 20 February: The forth day of the Chinese New Year, a three-day weekend.
  • 6 April: Tomb Sweeping Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 19 June: Dragon Boat Festival, a three-day weekend.
  • 25 September: Mid-Autumn Festival, a three-day weekend.
