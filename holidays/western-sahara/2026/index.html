Long weekends and bridges

  • 27 February: Independence Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 20 March: End of Ramadan (Eid al-Fitr), a three-day weekend.
  • 9 June: Day of the Martyrs is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 16 June: Islamic New Year is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 25 August: Birthday of Muhammad (Mawlid) is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
