Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 18 January: Martin Luther King Jr. Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 15 February: Washington's Birthday, a three-day weekend.
  • 31 May: Memorial Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 18 June: Juneteenth, a three-day weekend.
  • 5 July: Independence Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 6 September: Labour Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 11 October: Columbus Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 11 November: Veterans Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 25 November: Thanksgiving Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 24 December: Christmas Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 31 December: New Year's Day, a three-day weekend.
