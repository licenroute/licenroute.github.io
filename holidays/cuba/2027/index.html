Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: Triumph of the Revolution, a three-day weekend.
  • 26 March: Good Friday, a three-day weekend.
  • 26 July: Commemoration of the Assault of the Moncada garrison, a three-day weekend.
  • 27 July: Day after the Commemoration of the Assault of the Moncada garrison is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
