Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 3 April: Good Friday, a three-day weekend.
  • 1 May: Labour Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 21 May: Navy Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 29 June: Saints Peter and Paul, a three-day weekend.
  • 16 July: Our Lady of Mount Carmel is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 18 September: National holiday, a three-day weekend.
  • 12 October: Columbus Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 8 December: Immaculate Conception is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 25 December: Christmas Day, a three-day weekend.
