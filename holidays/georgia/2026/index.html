Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 2 January: Bedoba, a three-day weekend.
  • 19 January: Orthodox Epiphany, a three-day weekend.
  • 3 March: Mother's Day is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 9 April: National Unity Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 10 April: Good Friday, a three-day weekend.
  • 13 April: Easter Monday, a three-day weekend.
  • 12 May: Saint Andrew the First-Called Day is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 26 May: Independence Day is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 28 August: Saint Mary's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 23 November: Saint George's Day, a three-day weekend.
