Long weekends and bridges

  • 2 April: Passover (Pesach) is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 22 May: Shavuot (Feast of Weeks), a three-day weekend.
  • 21 September: Day of Atonement (Yom Kippur), a three-day weekend.
