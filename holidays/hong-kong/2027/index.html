Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: The first day of January, a three-day weekend.
  • 8 February: The third day of Lunar New Year, a three-day weekend.
  • 9 February: The fourth day of Lunar New Year is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 26 March: Good Friday, a three-day weekend.
  • 29 March: Easter Monday, a three-day weekend.
  • 5 April: Ching Ming Festival, a three-day weekend.
  • 13 May: Birthday of the Buddha is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 1 July: Hong Kong Special Administrative Region Establishment Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 16 September: The day following the Chinese Mid-Autumn Festival is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 1 October: National Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 8 October: Chung Yeung Festival, a three-day weekend.
  • 27 December: The first weekday after Christmas Day, a three-day weekend.
