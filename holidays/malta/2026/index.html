Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 10 February: Feast of Saint Paul's Shipwreck in Malta is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 19 March: Saint Joseph is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 31 March: Freedom Day is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 3 April: Good Friday, a three-day weekend.
  • 1 May: Labour Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 29 June: Saints Peter and Paul, a three-day weekend.
  • 8 September: Victory Day is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 21 September: Independence Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 8 December: Immaculate Conception is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 25 December: Christmas Day, a three-day weekend.
