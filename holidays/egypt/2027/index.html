Long weekends and bridges

  • 7 January: Christmas Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 25 January: National Police Day / Revolution Day 2011, a three-day weekend.
  • 9 March: End of Ramadan (Eid al-Fitr) is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 3 May: Sham El Nessim, a three-day weekend.
  • 23 July: Revolution Day, a three-day weekend.
