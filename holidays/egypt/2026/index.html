Long weekends and bridges

  • 20 March: End of Ramadan (Eid al-Fitr), a three-day weekend.
  • 13 April: Sham El Nessim, a three-day weekend.
  • 1 May: Labour Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 16 June: Islamic New Year is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 30 June: 30 June Day is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 23 July: Revolution Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 25 August: Birthday of Muhammad (Mawlid) is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 6 October: Armed Forces Day is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
