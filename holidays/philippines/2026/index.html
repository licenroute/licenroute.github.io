Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 20 March: End of Ramadan (Eid al-Fitr), a three-day weekend.
  • 2 April: Maundy Thursday is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 3 April: Good Friday, a three-day weekend.
  • 9 April: Day of Valor is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 1 May: Labour Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 12 June: Independence Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 31 August: National Heroes' Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 30 November: Bonifacio Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 25 December: Christmas Day, a three-day weekend.
