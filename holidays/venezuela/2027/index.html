Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 8 February: Carnival, a three-day weekend.
  • 9 February: Shrove Tuesday is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 25 March: Maundy Thursday is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 26 March: Good Friday, a three-day weekend.
  • 19 April: Declaration of Independence, a three-day weekend.
  • 24 June: Battle of Carabobo is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 5 July: Independence Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 12 October: Day of Indigenous Resistance is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 24 December: Christmas Eve, a three-day weekend.
  • 31 December: New Year's Eve, a three-day weekend.
