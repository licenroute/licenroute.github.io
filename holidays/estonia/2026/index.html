Long weekends and bridges

  • 1 January: New Year's Day is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 24 February: Independence Day is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 3 April: Good Friday, a three-day weekend.
  • 1 May: Labour Day, a three-day weekend.
  • 23 June: Victory Day is on a Tuesday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 20 August: Day of Restoration of Independence is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 24 December: Christmas Eve is on a Thursday; take one day off for a four-day weekend.
  • 25 December: Christmas Day, a three-day weekend.
