Spain VAT (IVA) and IRPF calculator for invoices

IVA, recargo de equivalencia, IRPF withholding and invoice total for Spain, to the cent, with the BOE rates.

Calculated in your browser with the same code as the API: nothing you type is sent anywhere.

How it works

  • IVA rates: 21% standard, 10% reduced, 4% super-reduced and 0%. Choose the one that applies to what you sell; the tool does not decide it for you.
  • IRPF withholding for freelancers (autónomos): 15% standard and 7% in the year you start and the next two (IRPF Regulation, art. 95).
  • Recargo de equivalencia (retailers): 5.2% with 21% IVA, 1.4% with 10% and 0.5% with 4%.
  • Valid for mainland Spain and the Balearic Islands. The Canary Islands (IGIC), Ceuta and Melilla (IPSI) have their own tax. Not tax advice.
